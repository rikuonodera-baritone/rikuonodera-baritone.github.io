BIOGRAPHY +

Riku Onodera

Japanese baritone currently based in Tokyo, pursuing a Master of Music in Vocal Music at Tokyo University of the Arts.
He holds a Bachelor of Music in Vocal Performance from Hokkaido University of Education. His repertoire encompasses opera, concert, and Lied repertoire.

His recent and upcoming performances include operatic roles, orchestral works, and song repertoire in Japan.
